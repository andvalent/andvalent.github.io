I help businesses turn scattered data — spreadsheets, CRMs, third-party exports — into reliable analytics infrastructure and clear, decision-ready reporting.

With a PhD and 9+ years of experience across fintech, telecoms, and consulting, I work at the intersection of data analysis and analytics engineering: building the pipelines and data models that make good analysis possible, then doing the analysis.

My current focus is on the modern open-source data stack — **dbt**, **Prefect**, **dlt**, **PostgreSQL** — deployed in production environments, not just notebooks.

---

## What I work on

**Analytics engineering** — designing and building end-to-end data pipelines: ingestion, transformation, modelling, automated reporting. Tools: dbt, dlt, Prefect, Airflow, PostgreSQL.

**Data analysis** — sales funnel analysis, cohort analysis, time-series, anomaly detection, stakeholder reporting. Tools: SQL, Python (Pandas, NumPy), Power BI, Tableau.

**Data infrastructure for small teams** — helping companies with little or no data function go from zero to a functioning, maintainable analytics stack.

**Advanced analytics & machine learning** — classification and regression models (tree-based: XGBoost, Random Forest), model evaluation and explainability (precision, recall, F1, feature importance). Applied to real-world problems including aircraft stand allocation, customer churn, and anomaly detection in financial transactions.
